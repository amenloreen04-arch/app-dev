# The Summer I Turned Pretty

> "It's going to be a hot girl summer in Cousins Beach."

## About the Series
***The Summer I Turned Pretty*** is a heartwarming coming-of-age television series based on the bestselling novel trilogy by Jenny Han. It follows Belly Conklin as she navigates first love, first heartbreak, and the magic of growing up during her family's annual summer trips to Cousins Beach.

### Key Highlights
* **Breathtaking Beach Scenery:** Set against the gorgeous, sun-soaked backdrop of Cousins Beach.
* **The Ultimate Love Triangle:** Belly caught between childhood friends and brothers, Conrad and Jeremiah.
* **Nostalgic Soundtrack:** Features amazing throwback hits and tracks by Taylor Swift.

### Main Characters
1. **Belly Conklin** - The heartwarming protagonist experiencing a transformative summer.
2. **Conrad Fisher** - The brooding, complex older brother with a hidden romantic side.
3. **Jeremiah Fisher** - The warm, golden-retriever-energy younger brother who is always there for Belly.
